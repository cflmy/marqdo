## 音频
    + `操作`="play"
    + `id`="default"
    + `源`=""
    + `频率`=None
    + `毫秒`=None
    + `选择器`=""

构造 audio 效应。

*`spec` = > table.put in=None at="op" value=操作*
*`spec` = > table.put in=`spec` at="id" value=id*
1. `源` != ""
    *`spec` = > table.put in=`spec` at="src" value=源*
1. `频率` != None
    *`spec` = > table.put in=`spec` at="freq" value=频率*
1. `毫秒` != None
    *`spec` = > table.put in=`spec` at="ms" value=毫秒*
1. `选择器` != ""
    *`spec` = > table.put in=`spec` at="sel" value=选择器*
**> table.put in=None at="audio" value=spec**
